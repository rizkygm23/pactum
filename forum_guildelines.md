What to post to the Ecosystem Showcase?
Ecosystem Showcase & Launches
Post in Ecosystem Showcase like you are shipping a practical “how to use this” guide.  Preference towards “How to use this as a developer” or “How to integrate this protocol”
﻿
We would love to see what you're building, and how it works! You can also embed videos but you must upload them to Youtube or Vimeo First.  Use the Film Icon to embed a video!
﻿
Do not post random AI generated content about websites you don't know about and then open support requests asking why your post won't work.  There are real users on these forums who want to see real content.  
﻿
Do not make a new thread for every update that you want to post please.  Instead, update the main post about your project.  This will allow it to be more visible to everyone, and for everyones posts to be more visible to each other.
﻿
Your post should be technical enough to be credible, while also being short enough that someone reads it. Remember: this forum has real users and builders who will try what you post.
﻿
If your post won't go through, take a look at what you're writing carefully.  Do not use vercel or other free webhost domains as there is a high likely hood it doesn't work!
﻿
Examples: keep it tight:
Titles: 
“[Project] on Arc: what it does”
“How to integrate [Project] as a developer”
“Announcing a new project on testnet: [Project]”
﻿
TL;DR: 3 bullet points *Do not post only a TL;DR or it's not TL Got it?
What it is
Who it’s for
What you can do today
How to Use it
Where to find it
Can you build against it?
﻿
What it does: 2-4 sentences in plain language (no hype)
﻿
Quickstart: shortest path to a working result (install/setup + one example call/flow + expected output). Link to full docs/examples instead of pasting huge code.
﻿
Integration notes: 3-6 bullets on how it plugs into real stacks (SDK languages, API/webhooks, wallets/auth, payments/USDC flows, indexing/analytics, CI/templates, frameworks like Next.js/React/Node).
﻿
Security/limits: 3-5 bullets 
Ready to Launch vs experimental
Key management model
Known constraints
Audit status if any.
﻿
Links: 
Website
Docs
GitHub Repo (Highly preferred) 
Demo/screen recording (Use the
﻿
Media (strongly recommended):
1-3 screenshots of the “it works” moment.
30-120s screen recording demo: setup -> run -> result.
﻿
Persona targeting (one sentence each, max):
Builders: how to integrate + first win.
Founders/PMs: time-to-integrate + what feature it enables.
Infra/tooling: deployment model + reliability hooks.
Community/educators: easiest demo path + tutorial friendliness.
End Users: An easy way to experience your product safely.
Close with 2 specific questions to drive replies (stack used, blocker hit, desired integration).
﻿
Post updates by editing your thread and replying to comments!
